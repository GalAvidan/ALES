# Storyboard Intent

Scene-by-scene emotional and visual intent. This is the *why* behind each scene. For the *what*
(durations, asset refs, sync points), see `map/scenes.json`.

---

## Scene 1 — Problem (0:00–0:06)

**Mood:** Frustration, relatable chaos  
**Visual intent:** Quick cuts of the problem in everyday life. High energy, slightly frantic pacing.  
**Emotional beat:** Viewer thinks "yes, that's me."

---

## Scene 2 — Transition (0:06–0:10)

**Mood:** Anticipation, breath-hold  
**Visual intent:** Screen goes dark. Single particle of light grows.  
**Emotional beat:** Curiosity. Something is about to happen.

---

## Scene 3 — Product Reveal (0:10–0:18)

**Mood:** Relief, delight, "aha"  
**Visual intent:** Product emerges from the particle in a slow zoom — not a cut. The slow zoom is
intentional: it gives the viewer time to register what they're seeing.
See `decisions/slow-zoom-not-cut-scene3.md` for the rationale.  
**Emotional beat:** Satisfaction. Problem solved.

---

## Scene 4 — Benefit Demo (0:18–0:26)

**Mood:** Confidence, ease  
**Visual intent:** Product in use. Clean, spacious composition. One action, one outcome.  
**Emotional beat:** "I want that."

---

## Scene 5 — Logo Hold (0:26–0:30)

**Mood:** Trust, brand recognition  
**Visual intent:** White background, logo centered, tagline below. Static.  
**Emotional beat:** Brand imprint. Clean exit.
