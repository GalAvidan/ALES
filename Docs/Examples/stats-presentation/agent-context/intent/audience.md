# Audience

## Primary

- **CFO** — financially fluent, wants to see trend lines and variance from plan.
- **Board members (non-technical)** — need the story, not the methodology.

## Secondary

- **IR team** — will reuse charts in the shareholder letter; must be export-ready at 300 dpi.

## Rules Derived from Audience

- No statistical jargon (p-values, confidence intervals) in slide body text.
  Methodology footnotes are acceptable on appendix slides only.
- Avoid absolute large numbers without context — always pair with YoY or vs-plan delta.
- Use positive framing where accurate ("recovered to €X" not "loss narrowed to €X").
