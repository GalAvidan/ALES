# Narrative Arc

The deck tells a three-act story:

## Act 1 — Context (slides 1–4)

Set the scene: Q1 was hard, here is what happened and why.

- Slide 1: Title
- Slide 2: Q1 headwinds (macro + one-time factors)
- Slide 3: Revenue waterfall Q1 → Q3
- Slide 4: Market share held despite revenue dip (confidence signal)

## Act 2 — Recovery (slides 5–12)

Show the three recovery drivers with evidence.

- Slides 5–6: Driver 1 — Pricing uplift
- Slides 7–8: Driver 2 — New customer cohort (B2B segment)
- Slides 9–10: Driver 3 — Cost-to-serve reduction improving margin
- Slides 11–12: Combined effect — Q3 revenue and EBITDA vs plan

## Act 3 — Forward Look (slides 13–16)

Q4 guidance and risks.

- Slide 13: Q4 revenue guidance range
- Slide 14: Key assumptions
- Slide 15: Downside scenarios
- Slide 16: Closing / ask

## Appendix (slides 17–20)

Methodology, raw data tables, definitions.
