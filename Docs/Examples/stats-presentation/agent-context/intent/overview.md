# Project Overview

**Deck:** Q3 2026 Board Presentation — Revenue Recovery Story

## Purpose

Show the CFO and board that revenue has recovered after the Q1 dip, explain the three drivers behind
the recovery, and set expectations for Q4 guidance.

## Success Criteria

- Every attendee leaves knowing: revenue is back on track, why, and what to watch in Q4.
- No slide requires the presenter to explain a chart. Every chart must be self-explanatory.

## Constraints

- Max 20 slides (board attention budget).
- No raw data tables visible to audience — summarized values only.
- All numbers in EUR, one decimal place.
